For a specific prospect, switch to its preview/company-name branch before uploading. Keep main as the generic starter. Only upload photographs you have permission to use; do not present concept imagery as completed client projects.

Avoid unnecessarily large originals and remove private information before upload. Do not upload confidential material to a public repository.
